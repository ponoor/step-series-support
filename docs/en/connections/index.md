# Connections and settings

This section covers what you do before the first motion: wiring the power supply, motors, switches and the network, and setting up the board. It has the procedures and the wiring; how the functions work is explained in the [function guides](../guides/index.md).

## Pages {#pages}

- [Power supply and motor](power-supply-and-motor.md): power inputs, motor terminals, and how to choose a power supply and a motor.
- [Sensors and switches](sensors-and-switches.md): the HOME and LIMIT terminals, their pin assignment, and how to wire switches and sensors.
- [Network and the DIP switch](network-and-dip-switch.md): the ID, the initial network settings, and the MAC address.
- [Electromagnetic brake wiring](electromagnetic-brake-wiring.md): the brake output of the STEP800, and the wiring of the EMB-STEP400 for the STEP400.
- [How the settings work](how-settings-work.md): where the settings are, when they take effect, and where they are saved.
- [Configuration with a microSD card](microsd-card.md): preparing the card and the configuration file.
- [Browser configuration tool](browser-config-tool.md): reading, editing and saving the settings over USB.
- [Configuration items](configuration-items.md): all the items of the configuration file with their initial values and ranges.
- [Example parameter values for example steppers](example-parameters.md): configuration files for some motors.
