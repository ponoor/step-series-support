# STEP Series

The STEP Series are stepper motor drivers that you control over Ethernet with OSC (Open Sound Control) messages. The STEP400 drives 4 motors and the STEP800 drives 8 motors; the STEP100 and the STEP200 are do-it-yourself versions with 1 and 2 motors.

This documentation describes the firmware **v2.2.0**. If you use an older version, read the [Migration guide](maintenance/migration-guide.md) before you update.

If this is your first time, read the [Overview](getting-started/index.md), and then follow the [Quick start](getting-started/quickstart.md) to turn a motor.

## What are you here for? {#sections}

- **To make it turn for the first time:** [Overview](getting-started/index.md) (the products, what you can do, the software to send OSC) and [Quick start](getting-started/quickstart.md) (one motor, step by step).
- **To wire it and set it up:** [Connections and settings](connections/index.md). The power supply and the motor, the sensors and switches, the network and the DIP switch, the electromagnetic brake, and the configuration with a microSD card or the browser configuration tool.
- **To understand how a function works:** [Functional guides](guides/index.md). Communication, voltage mode and current mode, the speed profile, homing, the servo mode, the electromagnetic brake, and the alarms.
- **To look up a command:** [OSC reference](reference/index.md) and the [Command list](reference/command-list.md). The arguments, the ranges, the initial values and the replies of each command.
- **To check the board itself:** [Hardware](hardware/step400.md) ([STEP400](hardware/step400.md), [STEP800](hardware/step800.md), [EMB-STEP400](hardware/emb-step400.md)): the dimensions, the pin assignment and the main components.
- **To update the firmware or to find a cause:** [Maintenance](maintenance/index.md). The firmware update, building the firmware, the diagnosis via USB serial port, the troubleshooting, the changelog and the migration guide.
- **To build a STEP100 or a STEP200:** [STEP100 / STEP200](step100-200/index.md). The materials, the assembly and the firmware.
